Notes: single threaded. "i8 flat" holds one row of A against four rows of
B; "i8 blocked" holds four against four, so B is fetched once per block of
output rows rather than once per row. "i8 smmla" is that same 4x4 block with
SMMLA in place of SDOT -- 32 multiply-accumulates per instruction instead of
16, from the same eight loads. "vs blk" isolates the instruction change.
M=1 has no row reuse to recover and is expected to be flat. All kernels
share the transposed-B layout. Not a comparison to a tuned BLAS.
